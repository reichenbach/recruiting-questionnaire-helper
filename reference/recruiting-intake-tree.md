# College Recruiting Questionnaire – Intake Question Tree
Built Oct 23, 2026 (CT) from real recruiting questionnaires. **Read-only survey:** nothing was started, continued, or submitted. ARMS field definitions were taken from the public JSON that every ARMS form loads (`/arms/api/public/questionnaires/<hash>`). FrontRush, JumpForward and school-built forms were read from page HTML. Only fields actually seen are listed.

**Sample:** 86 ARMS forms in the first pass, plus 18 ARMS forms and 16 school-built forms added Oct 23 (3 more could not be read) (the frequency counts in section 1 cover only the first 86 ARMS forms). The first pass also had 2 FrontRush forms (live; 2 more were expired), 1 JumpForward form (most JumpForward links now redirect to ARMS), and 5 school-built forms (PrestoSports-hosted and Slate). Platform share: ARMS strongly dominates, so the tree is ARMS-heavy.
Legend: **REQ** = required on at least one form (counts given where useful); *opt* = seen, never or rarely required.

## 3. Platform quirks
**ARMS (questionnaires.armssoftware.com)**
- The landing page offers Google/Twitter sign-in or "Continue without". **Continue creates a profile** in the school's ARMS, so do it only with approval.
- Phone fields need 10 digits. The 555-0101 test number was rejected.
- **New high school (not in lookup):** name, street address line 1 and state are REQ in the schema. ZIP is not flagged REQ in the schema but was enforced in practice on Lewis. **"Does your school have an American Football Team?"** is REQ (in the school block on 85/86 forms). Collect the HS street address, city, state, ZIP and has-football Y/N up front.
- Position dropdowns are frequently empty (21/86). Some option lists are messy free-text values (UIndy and GateWay soccer, CSU Pueblo W lacrosse), so pick the closest clean value.
- Many forms ask nothing sport-specific beyond the default template. Metrics come from per-school custom fields, so availability varies.
- The NCAA sexual-misconduct attestation is required wherever present.
- Repeatable blocks (parents, coaches, club teams, tournaments) use "add" dialogs. Club teams and tournaments use search lookups.
- File uploads: transcript, test scores, headshot.
- Some JumpForward URLs (college.jumpforward.com/questionnaire.aspx) now just point to an ARMS link.

**FrontRush (frontrush.com/FR_Web_App/Player/PlayerSubmit.aspx?sid=...&ptype=recruit)**
- One page with a **captcha** before submit ("Please validate captcha").
- Required (*): first and last name, email, ZIP, cell phone, grad year (the volleyball form also requires city, state, high school, HS coach name, HS position and HS jersey #).
- **Required NCSA and IMG Academy marketing consent radios (Yes/No).** Always answer "No thanks" unless the family opts in.
- Phone numbers must be 10 digits. "Personal Contacts" is a repeatable block (mother, father, coach...).
- The high school block has an "is not listed" fallback that expands to address 1–3, city, state, ZIP, country and CEEB.
- Links expire. Grossmont College swim (sid=MjA3NTc=…), CWRU (sid 1536) and CCBC Essex said "This questionnaire is no longer available".

**JumpForward (college.jumpforward.com/questionnaire.aspx?iid=..&sportid=..)**
- Long single page (needs JavaScript): personal info, Parent 1, Parent 2, guardian, academics with a full school address, phone, fax and counselor block, then athletic info and fun "Other Questions".
- Many schools have migrated to ARMS.

**School-built**
- PrestoSports "Recruit Questionnaire" forms (Central Methodist, College of Saint Mary, IU East, Culver-Stockton, Tennessee Wesleyan, NIACC, Genesee, Anne Arundel, Itawamba, Holmes) share one template: often only email marked *, an optional secondary email, "Married?", parents' occupations, a previous-college block and the boilerplate NAIA ID paragraph. JUCO versions (Genesee, Holmes) add a **reCAPTCHA**. Section headers are sometimes copy-pasted wrong (the TWU women's form says "Men's Lacrosse").
- Slate forms (Graceland, St. Norbert): St. Norbert has hidden "Sport (Internal Only)" and "Rating (Internal Only)" fields (leave them alone). Its text-message question is REQ.
- PrestoSports forms (Central Methodist, NAIA): REQ name, address 1, city, state, ZIP, email and cell. They have previous-college and eligibility-remaining fields and an NAIA ID field.
- Slate forms (Graceland, NAIA): need JavaScript. They ask first-year vs transfer, start term, campus, academic interest and gender. A cell number triggers SMS/AI-contact consent text.

## 4. Intake order
1. **Sport + gender team + grad year + target levels** (NCAA D1/D2/D3, NAIA, JUCO). This decides the branch and whether to ask for the NCAA ID or NAIA ID.
2. **Athlete:** legal and preferred name, email (plus secondary), 10-digit cell, home phone, full home address with ZIP, DOB, gender, citizenship, X/Instagram handles, headshot file.
3. **Parents/guardians (1 and 2):** name, relationship, email, 10-digit cell, occupation, college attended, highest education, address if different; lives with, marital status (optional), siblings and ages.
4. **High school:** name, **street address, city, state, ZIP, phone, CEEB code, has-football Y/N**; HS coach name, phone and email; counselor name, phone and email.
5. **Academics:** GPA (weighted and unweighted), class rank, SAT/ACT scores and dates (planned dates if not taken), intended major, AP courses, honors, transcript and test score files; entry term, application status, ED/financial aid interest; transfer details if applicable.
6. **Eligibility:** NCAA Eligibility Center registered Y/N plus NCAA ID; NAIA ID if NAIA.
7. **Common athletic:** height, weight, video links (Hudl/YouTube), recruiting profile URL, club team, club coach and contact, club jersey #, upcoming tournaments and showcases, other sports, athletic honors, other schools considering.
8. **Sport-specific block (section 2) for the named sport only.**
9. **Consents & attestations:** see the subsection below. Show the exact wording, use only the family's answer, marketing/SMS default No.

### Consents & attestations: never answered for the family
These are legal attestations or marketing/contact consents, so **never pick an answer for the family**. For each one, show the family the exact wording, record only their answer, and leave it blank (do not submit) if they haven't answered. **Marketing and contact consents default to No / "No thanks"** unless the family explicitly says Yes.

1. **NCAA Sexual Misconduct Attestation** (ARMS, required wherever present; Amherst wording shown). Show the full school paragraph, including the glossary and the note that the college may run a criminal records check. Options:
   - "1. I attest that I HAVE NOT been subject to a final finding" (full text: "...for violating a high school, college, or university rule(s) regarding Title IX sexual harassment or other sexual misconduct. I further attest that I HAVE NOT been criminally convicted for sexual violence, interpersonal violence, or other acts of violence. If I am a transfer student, I further attest that there IS NOT a pending disciplinary proceeding against me at any college or university that I previously attended.")
   - "2. I attest that I HAVE been subject to a final finding" (or convicted, or has a pending proceeding as a transfer).
2. **FrontRush: NCSA College Recruiting** (REQ Yes/No): "By selecting Yes and clicking Submit, I provide my electronic signature and consent, as parent/legal guardian and on behalf of my child, to share personal information with and be contacted by NCSA and IMG Academy at the number(s) provided via recurring calls or texts, including by autodialer, AI, or prerecorded voice, and to receive a free profile. Consent is not required for purchase. Revoke consent: 1-877-845-6272. I agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use." Choices: "Yes, I want to maximize my recruiting! Email me a free recruiting profile to get started." / **"No thanks" (default)**.
3. **FrontRush: IMG Academy Camps** (REQ Yes/No): same wording, but "...contacted by IMG Academy and its affiliates...". Choices: "Yes, I am interested in hearing more about camp programs available throughout the year." / **"No thanks" (default)**.
4. **SMS / AI contact.** Graceland (Slate): "By providing a mobile phone number, you authorize Graceland University to contact you via calls and text/SMS, including AI-generated content. Message/data rates may apply. Text STOP anytime to Opt-Out." plus "I would like to receive SMS/text messages from Graceland University about important updates via text messaging (message and data rates may apply).
